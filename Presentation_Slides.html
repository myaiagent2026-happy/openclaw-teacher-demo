📚 Breaking Down the Word

PHOTO = Light ☀️

SYNTHESIS = Making/Building 🔨

PHOTOSYNTHESIS = Making food using light!

🏭

Plants are Like Food Factories!

Imagine a factory that:

  • Takes in raw materials
  • Uses energy (sunlight)
  • Produces food (glucose)
  • Gives out oxygen

That's a plant!

🧪 What Do Plants Need?

Three main ingredients:

  • 1️⃣ Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) - from the air
  • 2️⃣ Water (H₂O) - from the soil
  • 3️⃣ Sunlight - from the sun ☀️

Plus: Chlorophyll (green color in leaves)

🍃

In the Leaves!

Why are leaves green?

Because they contain chlorophyll!

Chlorophyll acts like solar panels - it captures sunlight!

🔄 How Photosynthesis Works

  1. Roots absorb water from soil
  2. Leaves take in carbon dioxide from air
  3. Chlorophyll captures sunlight
  4. Plant uses energy to convert water + CO₂ into glucose
  5. Oxygen is released as a byproduct

⚗️ Photosynthesis Equation

Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight

↓

GLUCOSE (food) + OXYGEN

Word Equation:
CO₂ + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen

🍬

The Food Plants Make

Glucose is used for:

  • ✅ Energy (like how we use food)
  • ✅ Growth (building new leaves, stems, roots)
  • ✅ Storage (storing as starch for later)

🌍 Plants Help Us!

Plants give us:

💨

Oxygen - we breathe it!

🍎

Food - fruits, vegetables, grains

🌬️

Clean air - remove CO₂

🏠

Homes - for animals

Without plants, there would be no life on Earth!

🌟 Fun Facts!

🌿

Plants make enough oxygen for all living things

🌳

One large tree can provide oxygen for 4 people

🌊

Algae in the ocean make 50% of Earth's oxygen

⏰

Plants have been making food for 3 billion years!
