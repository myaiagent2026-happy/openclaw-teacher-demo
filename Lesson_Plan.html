📋 Lesson Plan: Photosynthesis

Grade Level: Primary 6 (11-12 years old)

Subject: Science

Duration: 60 minutes

Topic: Photosynthesis - How Plants Make Food

📚 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Explain what photosynthesis is in simple terms
  2. Identify the three main ingredients plants need for photosynthesis
  3. Describe the role of chlorophyll and sunlight
  4. Write the basic word equation for photosynthesis
  5. Understand why plants are important for all living things

🎯 Success Criteria

  • ✅ I can explain what photosynthesis means
  • ✅ I can name the three things plants need to make food
  • ✅ I can write the photosynthesis equation
  • ✅ I can explain why plants are called "food factories"

📖 Lesson Structure

Introduction (10 minutes)

  • Hook: Show a time-lapse video of a plant growing
  • Ask: "Where does the plant get its food from?"
  • Discuss: Student ideas and misconceptions

Main Teaching (25 minutes)

1. What is photosynthesis?

  • "Photo" = light, "synthesis" = making
  • Plants make their own food using sunlight!

2. The Recipe for Photosynthesis

  • Carbon dioxide (from air)
  • Water (from roots)
  • Sunlight (energy source)
  • Chlorophyll (green pigment in leaves)

3. The Equation (simplified)

Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose (food) + Oxygen

4. Why are plants important?

  • Produce oxygen for us to breathe
  • Base of all food chains
  • Remove CO₂ from atmosphere

Activity (15 minutes)

  • Worksheet completion (individual/pairs)
  • Role-play: Students act as "ingredients" and become "products"

Plenary (10 minutes)

  • Review learning objectives
  • Exit ticket quiz
  • Preview next lesson

🛠️ Materials Needed

  • Whiteboard/projector
  • Photosynthesis diagram poster
  • Worksheet (one per student)
  • Quiz (one per student)
  • Real plant specimen (optional)
  • Colored pencils

📝 Key Vocabulary

Word Definition
Photosynthesis The process by which plants make food using sunlight
Chlorophyll Green pigment in leaves that captures sunlight
Carbon Dioxide Gas from the air that plants use
Glucose Sugar that plants make (their food)
Oxygen Gas released by plants during photosynthesis

🎨 Differentiation

Support:

  • Provide word bank for worksheet
  • Pair with stronger student
  • Use visual aids and diagrams

Extension:

  • Write the chemical equation
  • Research how cacti photosynthesize in deserts
  • Create a poster explaining photosynthesis

📊 Assessment

Formative:

  • Q&A during lesson
  • Worksheet completion
  • Exit ticket quiz

Summative:

  • End-of-unit test
  • Photosynthesis project

🏠 Homework Ideas

  1. Draw a diagram of photosynthesis
  2. Write a story from a leaf's perspective
  3. Research: "Do all plants photosynthesize the same way?"
