Lesson Plan: Photosynthesis

Grade Level Secondary 2 (13-14 years)
Subject Integrated Science
Duration 60 minutes
Topic Photosynthesis - Energy Conversion in Plants

Learning Objectives

By the conclusion of this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Define photosynthesis and explain its biological significance
  2. Identify and describe the role of essential components: carbon dioxide, water, chlorophyll, and light energy
  3. Construct the word equation for photosynthesis and explain the energy transformation
  4. Analyze the relationship between photosynthesis and cellular respiration
  5. Evaluate the ecological importance of photosynthesis in maintaining atmospheric balance

Success Criteria

  • Accurately define photosynthesis using appropriate scientific terminology
  • Identify the reactants and products of the photosynthetic process
  • Construct a balanced word equation representing photosynthesis
  • Explain the role of plants as primary producers in ecosystems

Lesson Structure

Introduction (10 minutes)

  • Engagement: Present time-lapse video demonstrating plant growth
  • Diagnostic Assessment: Elicit prior knowledge through guided questioning
  • Learning Focus: Establish relevance to daily life and ecological systems

Core Instruction (25 minutes)

1. Conceptual Framework

  • Etymology: Photo- (light) + synthesis (composition)
  • Classification as an anabolic, endothermic process
  • Distinction from heterotrophic nutrition

2. Process Requirements

  • Carbon dioxide: Atmospheric source and stomatal uptake
  • Water: Root absorption and xylem transport
  • Light energy: Electromagnetic spectrum (400-700nm)
  • Chlorophyll: Pigment structure and light absorption

3. Chemical Equation

Carbon Dioxide + Water + Light Energy → Glucose + Oxygen

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light Energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

4. Ecological Significance

  • Oxygen production for aerobic respiration
  • Carbon cycle regulation
  • Foundation of food chains (primary production)

Application Activity (15 minutes)

  • Structured worksheet completion (individual/paired work)
  • Concept mapping: Linking reactants, process, and products
  • Data interpretation: Analyzing photosynthesis rate graphs

Consolidation (10 minutes)

  • Learning objective review
  • Formative assessment: Exit ticket
  • Preview of related topic: Cellular respiration

Required Materials

Key Terminology

Term Definition
Photosynthesis The biochemical process by which photoautotrophs convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose
Chlorophyll Green pigment located in chloroplasts that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis
Carbon Dioxide Atmospheric gas (CO₂) absorbed through stomata as a reactant in photosynthesis
Glucose Simple carbohydrate (C₆H₁₂O₆) produced as the primary product of photosynthesis
Oxygen Diatomic gas (O₂) released as a byproduct of the light-dependent reactions
